How to study this

Know the core cold. It never moves with sizing, so it costs nothing to memorise once.

Learn the middle as buckets, not frequencies. Pairs, suited Ax, offsuit Ax, broadways, the 9s and 8s ladders. Smaller open, call more. Larger open, 3-bet more. Suited broadways swing hardest.

Spend the time on the bottom. Switch between the four open sizes with Isolate what moved on: the handful of cells that light up are the entire practical adjustment.

Four things to re-check

Transcribed straight from your notes, so these are shown as given, but they run against the usual shape and may be typos in the source:

1. Versus a 2x button you defend 2-gappers 85s+, tighter than the 74s+ you use against CO and EP. The widest opener normally earns the widest defence.

2. CO and EP both hold 1-gappers at 53s+ for 2x and 2.5x, while the button tightens to 75s+ at 2.5x.

3. Against CO the suited connector floor sits at 54s+ for every size from 2.5x up, so that row never moves.

4. The 9s ladder against BU and CO reads K9s / Q9s / J9s with no T9s, while the 8s ladder below it does include T8s. That puts T9s beneath T8s on the grid. Against EP your notes do list T9s, so this looks like a dropped hand rather than a real read.
